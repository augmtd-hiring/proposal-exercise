# Acme Advisory: standard terms (2026)

## Fees and pricing rules
- Fees are quoted in EUR, based on the rate card in force when the proposal is issued.
- Engagements above €12,000 before VAT receive a **10% discount** on fees.
- VAT at **23%** is added to all fees and shown separately.
- Travel and accommodation outside Lisbon are billed at cost and are not included in quoted fees.

## Invoicing and payment
- 50% of fees invoiced at kick-off, 50% on delivery of the final output.
- Payment within 30 days of invoice date.

## Validity
- Proposals are valid for 30 days from the date of issue.

## Changes and cancellation
- Changes to scope are agreed in writing and may change fees.
- Workshops cancelled less than 5 working days before the session are billed in full.

## Confidentiality
- Both parties keep each other's information confidential. We never share one client's information
  with another client.

## Liability
- Our total liability is limited to the fees paid under the engagement.
