# Globex Retail: proposal for Project Falcon (store replenishment)

*Prepared by Acme Advisory for Dana Whitfield, COO, 14 November 2025*

## 1. Context
Globex Retail runs 42 stores supplied from one central depot. Store managers told us shelves run empty
on Mondays and overstock builds up on Thursdays, and that replenishment orders are still placed by
phone. Dana's goal is to cut emergency deliveries in half before the spring season.

## 2. Objectives
- One replenishment rhythm all 42 stores follow
- Emergency deliveries down by 50% within six months
- A clear owner for replenishment at the depot

## 3. Scope of work
**Operations diagnostic.** Two days at the depot and three stores, interviews, and a review of twelve
months of order data.

**Three process redesign workshops.** Ordering rhythm, depot picking, store receiving.

**Written recommendations.** Prioritised actions, owners, and a 90-day plan.

**Board pack.** A summary for the Globex board meeting in February.

## 4. Implementation timeline
- Weeks 1–2: diagnostic
- Weeks 3–5: workshops
- Week 6: recommendations and board pack
