*Prepared by Acme Advisory for Raj Mehta, Head of Quality, 9 March 2026*

## 1. Context
Initech's batch release takes eleven days on average against a target of five. Raj described a
release process where documents wait in three inboxes, and an audit finding from January that the
team must close by June. The work must not disrupt the validated production schedule.

## 2. Objectives
- Batch release in five days or less
- The January audit finding closed with evidence
- A release checklist the quality team owns

## 3. Scope of work
**Two process redesign workshops.** Document flow; release decision meeting.

**Written recommendations.** Prioritised actions, owners, and a 90-day plan, written so it can be filed
as audit evidence.
