# Acme Advisory: services

We help operations teams in logistics, retail and manufacturing fix how work flows.

## What we offer

### Operations diagnostic
A structured review of one operation: two days on site, interviews with the team, and a review of the
operation's own data. **Standard effort: 4 consultant days + 1 partner day.** Output: a findings
briefing with the client's leadership.

### Process redesign workshops
Facilitated half-day workshops with the people who run the process, one topic per workshop. Each
session is run by a partner and a consultant, and includes preparation and materials. Priced per
session (see the rate card).

### Written recommendations
A written report with prioritised recommendations, owners and a 90-day plan, built on the diagnostic
and the workshops. **Standard effort: 2 consultant days.**

### Board pack
A short summary deck for a board or leadership team, plus one presentation by a partner. Fixed fee.

### Ongoing coaching
Monthly check-ins with the process owner after a project. Quoted separately on request.

## What we don't offer

- Payroll, HR administration or accounting services
- Software implementation or WMS configuration
- Recruitment or interim staffing

When a client asks for something on this list, we say so clearly and, where we can, suggest they
look for a specialist.
